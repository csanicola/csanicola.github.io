The way I was hungry with all the food descriptions, yet knowing it was being used for nefarious means...am I the bad person here?
Overall, since I knew the story behind the book, it wasn't all the surprising but I did wish for the ending to change from reality because it would have been more interesting. I did like how it was written and nobody is really the "good guy" in this story.

## Additional Notes 
- I had previously watched the [Rotten Mango YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S9rwmYN0LIM) that discussed this case (in addition to another similar serial killer) so I was familiar with the real story but this book still did a good job of me guessing what was going to happen next. 
- The description of food alone is this book is so mouthwatering that I immediately had to make rice and butter as soon as I was listening to it described in the book.
