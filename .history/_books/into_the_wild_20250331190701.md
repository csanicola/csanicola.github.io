This has been my top 5 movies of all time since I was in middle school. I watched the movie before I read the book but it didn't turn into the typical action of mine where as soon as I watch the movie I think I don't have to read the book. I was so wrong. I've always read books since I was young about people going off and trying to survive in the wild and it's always fascinated me. With this story, seeing how it ends for Chris makes it more realistic for what would happen if I were to attempt something like this so I'm glad I never did but will forever be fascinated by this story. I will live the rest of my life living vicariously through this story.
