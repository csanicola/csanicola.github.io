Project Hail Mary

Andy Weir  ·  2021

2026-03-31  ·  2026-04-13  ·   · 

2026  ·  family-bookclub  ·  science-fiction


Family Bookclub Handout - Book Review

Quick Warm-Up

  1. One word reaction: Science!
  2. Loved It Liked it Meh?
  3. Favorite character: Rocky, duh then Eva Stratt for being a boss bitch badass

“Wait…WHAT?!” Moments

  1. Biggest surprise: When you realize that Ryland was actually forced into the trip and that he was such a coward about it before hand
  2. Funniest scene: Probably Rocky learning sarcasm for the first time
  3. Most stressful moment: When they were exiting that other planets atmosphere and there is a hole in the ship and Rocky sacrifices himself for Ryland it was super stressful reading about it and really worrying how they were going to get out of it

Science (but fun)

  1. Cool Mind-blowing Too much?
  2. Explain astrophage in one sentence. The coolest made up space bacteria that I wish was real but probably better for humanity it isn’t.

Rocky Section

  1. Immediate love or slow burn? Immediate love
  2. Favorite Rocky moment: When Ryland comes back for Rocky after he is stranded and how happy and excited Rocky was to see his friend again - definitely gave me flashes of Theo being that excited when I come home after any time away
  3. Better partner: Rocky or human?

Would You Survive?

  1. Could you handle the mission? Absolutely not
  2. What would break you first? I’m not sure what would be the actual first thing but when he discovers that the astrophage enemy got into Rylands fuel, I would have not been able to bounce bake with a solution to that one as quickly
  3. Would you make the same choices? I would absolutely choose not to go back to Earth and to definitely go visit the alien planet with my new friend

Hot Takes

  1. Grace: hero or not? there were so many moments where he could have given up but he fought the entire time and eventually made sure to despite the choice to go save his friend, he made sure to send back his help to humanity too
  2. Stratt justified? yes, without her the project would not have happened with all the beauracrats getting in the way and the fact that she knew that the whole time and that most likely she will be jailed afterwards but still was giving it her all; also Ryland was the only obvious choice at the end to be included and I agreed with her decision that he had to go regardless of his feelings about it
  3. Did the ending work? it was the ending I wanted to happen so I am happy about it but there were too many variables at the end that could have very easily misaligned to have caused things to go horribly wrong

Rapid Fire

  1. MVP: Rocky
  2. Most stressful problem: the entire 3rd planet part collecting the enemy to the astrophage sample
  3. Best science moment: the initial lab scenes where no matter what science you are doing there are set steps and tests to perform to see how something works and what its limits are
  4. One thing you’d change: I don’t think I would change anything

Final Verdict

  1. Rating (1-5): 5
  2. Recommend? Why or why not? Do you like listening to Neil deGrasse Tyson videos and do you love science? Then you would love this book, otherwise just watch the movie.
