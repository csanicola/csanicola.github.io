The is a place that I will be a homebase to highlight all my current and past projects, publications I’ve been featured in, my thoughts in the form of a blog and just anything else I think is worth noting about myself and my work. Please don’t take anything I say too seriously because as a human I am constantly evolving and what I may believe today might not be the case tomorrow. All of my projects too can evolve at any time based on new technology/data so feel free to let me know your thoughts and comments on anything you see.

The best way to get in contact with me is via email but I can also be reached via my GitHub.

Digital Resources

I’ve created several digital resources to help researchers and analysts in their work. These include:

  • A comprehensive data analysis template for Excel
  • An automated Excel template for identifying comorbidities based on medication history
  • A statistical analysis toolkit with Python scripts

Visit my Resource Portal to learn more about these tools and how they can help with your research needs.
