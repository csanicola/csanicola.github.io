# Wrong Time, Right Place?
#technology #streaming/YouTube #reflection

_As humanity is on the decline in many ways, are there certain advantages of the times that are hindering rather than helping my capacities?_

In the back room of my house on a beautiful sunny summer day, sitting in the dark on my 5th YouTube videos of the night I suddenly took a moment to reflect that since middle school, I have had access to YouTube which since that day has comprised of an average of 4 hours of my day, everyday. 

Anytime I’m bored, or want to check in on my favorite YouTuber, or am watching a music video, there is always something I can click on. And I think about all that time as not only total hours but percentage of my life and think had I been born just a few years earlier, what would have taken up that time instead? Would I be doing more of my other hobbies like drawing/painting and reading or would I found and entirely different hobby I would have never discovered now? 

Should I feel grateful or regretful?
