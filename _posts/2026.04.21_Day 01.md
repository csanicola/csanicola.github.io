This is day one of trying to be a more active writer by starting with just putting down my thoughts in whatever form they appear in my head and in a mostly unedited way. I do know I have a tendency to write and speak in run on sentences so I will be trying to work on that. I was listening to a podcast recently where this was addressed. They talk about writing losing its cadence know and how words flow. Or poignant repetition. 
Eventually, I want to be writing more cohesive think pieces about things that interesting me but in which I am in no way an expert on. If I keep spending my entire life waiting until I become the most informed on something in order to not be wrong or misinformed, then I will never write anything in my life. /uplo
